## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, clinical-yet-welcoming healthcare aesthetic tailored for premium dental and oral care. The brand personality balances precision, cleanliness, and clinical authority with deep warmth, approachable hospitality, and reassuring comfort designed to dispel patient dental anxiety. 

The design combines **Minimalism** with **Tonal Layering and Soft Ambient Depth**:
- **Purity & Breathability:** Generous negative space, immaculate white canvas cards, and pale mist-slate surfaces establish clinical sterility without cold detachment.
- **Calming Confidence:** Saturated, disciplined medical navies project deep competence and stability, balanced by soothing sky and serene aqua accents that evoke hygiene, clear water, and painless care.
- **Tactile Softness:** Gentle continuous rounded corners and feather-light diffused shadows replace rigid corporate edges, signaling an empathetic, patient-first care journey.

## Colors

The color architecture is built for clinical legibility, emotional reassurance, and high-contrast accessibility across diverse age demographics.

- **Primary (`#0E2A47` - Deep Medical Navy):** Anchors high-priority interactive states, key typography, and top-tier navigation. Imparts authority, medical distinction, and rock-solid reliability.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` - Sky Cadence):** Communicates interactive clarity, progress states, patient calendar appointments, and key calls-to-action (booking, treatment reviews).
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` - Serene Cyan):** Represents gentle care, hygiene, vitality, and cosmetic dental wellness. Used for accents, subtle highlights, active badges, and patient success alerts.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Anchor):** Balanced slate scale that avoids stark carbon black. Provides soft body copy readability, precise input borders, and calming subheadings.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base Background: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for effortless eye comfort.
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) for pristine clinical framing and card elevation.
  - Subsurface / Tinted Wells: `#F0F9FF` (Sky 50) for treatment summaries, appointment notices, and highlighted patient advisories.

## Typography

The type system is powered universally by **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its rounded geometric apertures and friendly yet precise letterforms balance medical clarity with reassuring hospitality.

- **Headlines:** Set in Bold (`700`) and SemiBold (`600`) with tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to deliver crisp structure without looking harsh.
- **Body:** Sized generously (`15px` to `18px`) with relaxed line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide effortless scanning for patients reviewing dental procedures, pricing transparently, and treatment prep.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Crisp medium-to-bold weights with subtle tracking expansion for badge statuses, clinical metrics, and form field tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm using an adaptable fluid grid:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive layout with max container width constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading spans. Outer margins set to `3rem` (`48px`) with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (`32px`) margins and `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) side margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Interactive tap targets conform to minimum 48px heights.

Vertical spacing prioritizes spacious sections to convey relaxation, unhurried care, and clarity. Standard sectional padding adheres to `space-3xl` (64px) on mobile and `space-4xl` (96px) on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain an unblemished, sanitized, and gentle visual tone, elevation avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows. Depth is communicated through **ambient, blue-tinted diffusion** coupled with **whisper-soft hairline borders**.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Clinical Block):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Popovers):** Elevates floating elements (e.g. dentist bio cards, date pickers): `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.07), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Booking Bars):** High floating treatment dialogs and emergency contact overlays: `0 20px 35px -5px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.04)`.
- **Inner Depth (Tonal Well):** Inset background `#F1F5F9` with a delicate soft inner shadow for input fields and time-slot containers.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Roundedness Level 2** (`0.5rem` / `8px` base curve). 

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Minor Controls:** `rounded` (8px) ensures tactile, touch-friendly softness while preserving surgical structure.
- **Cards, Treatment Modules, and Consultation Blocks:** `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-xl` (24px), producing a plush, friendly posture that avoids aggressive acute corners.
- **Badges, Filter Pills, and Floating Status Tags:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to emphasize a modern cosmetic studio flair.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Medical Navy background (`#0E2A47`), pure white text, 8px border radius, 48px height for desktop. Subtle hover state transitioning to `#163A61` with subtle lift (`translate-y-[-1px]`).
- **Secondary / Action:** Sky Blue fill (`#0284C7`), pure white text. Used for primary conversion triggers (e.g., "Book Dental Appointment").
- **Outline / Gentle:** Pure white surface with `#E2E8F0` border, `#0E2A47` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F0F9FF` and border shifts to `#BAE6FD`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`, font size `12px` SemiBold.
  - *Confirmed / Available:* Sky Blue tint (`#E0F2FE` bg, `#0369A1` text).
  - *Hygiene / Clean Care:* Cyan tint (`#CFFAFE` bg, `#0E7490` text).
  - *Notice:* Soft Amber tint (`#FEF3C7` bg, `#92400E` text).

### Cards
- Pristine white background, 16px to 24px corner radii, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- Elevated via Level 1 ambient navy shadow.
- Interior layout incorporates ample padding (`24px` to `32px`) to separate treatment descriptions, doctor qualifications, and transparent pricing.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Default:** 44px height, 8px border radius, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `#0E2A47` text.
- **Focus:** Border transitions smoothly to `#0284C7` with a 3px serene halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.
- **Placeholder:** `#94A3B8` for calm readability.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px x 20px with 6px border radius (checkboxes) and circular radius (radios).
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked: `#0284C7` background, white check icon / radio dot, crisp transition.

### Lists & Schedule Items
- Clean divided rows separated by `#F1F5F9` border rules.
- Optional interactive item hover state with `#F8FAFC` surface fill and `#0284C7` left border accent indicator (3px).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Interactive Tooth Chart / Odontogram Nodes:** Clean geometric tooth selector tiles with state indicators (Healthy, Treated, Watchlist) using serene cyan and sky accents.
- **Before / After Smile Slider:** Crisp split-screen slider featuring a smooth 32px pill handle in Medical Navy with white dual-chevron indicator.
- **Doctor Credential Badge:** Soft avatar frame paired with clinical verified badge in cyan tint (`#06B6D4`).
